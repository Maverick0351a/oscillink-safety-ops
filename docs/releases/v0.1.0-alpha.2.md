The candidate was verified on Windows, a clean detached Buildbox Linux checkout, hosted CI on Ubuntu

## Assurance status and limitations

All results are synthetic or formal software evidence. The benchmark does not contain customer,
facility, equipment, incident, or field data. TLA+ proves only properties of its finite abstraction;
it is not a refinement proof against the Python runtime.

A represented healthy dependency remains unvalidated. Architectural independence, physical stopping,
stopping time, diagnostic coverage, PLr, SIL, application validation, certification, compliance, and
safe operation are not established.

Protective-stop and inhibit records remain local one-way simulated request artifacts. They do not
connect to a controller, acknowledge a request, or establish that equipment stopped. The project has
no machine, PLC, interlock, robot, actuator, remote-reset, or reverse-command interface.
