# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act in ways that support an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respect for differing views, constructive feedback,
accountability, and careful attention to the project's safety and authority boundaries.
Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized conduct, trolling, insults, threats, harassment,
publishing another person's private information, or other unprofessional conduct.

## Enforcement responsibilities and scope

Maintainers may clarify and enforce these standards and may remove, edit, or reject participation
that violates them. This Code applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the
project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer through a confidential repository collaboration
channel. Do not disclose sensitive personal information in a public issue. Maintainers will protect
reporter and affected-person privacy as far as reasonably possible. Responses may include a
correction, warning, temporary suspension, or permanent exclusion according to context and impact.
