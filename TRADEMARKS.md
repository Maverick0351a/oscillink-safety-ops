represented here as open source.

Oscillink Safety Ops” are permitted when they do not imply sponsorship, certification, partnership,
approval, or endorsement.

Forks and substantial modifications should use a distinct name and visual identity while retaining
required attribution and license notices. No logo or wordmark asset is licensed for separate
commercial branding use. Other uses require written permission from the trademark owner.
